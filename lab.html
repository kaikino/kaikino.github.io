Lab: a pipeline you can poke

A toy 5-stage in-order RISC-V pipeline (IF → ID → EX → MEM → WB) running a subset of RV32I, in your browser. Write some assembly, step it, and watch load-use stalls, forwarding and branch flushes happen in the diagram. It is a cartoon of KAISC-V, not the real thing.

program.s
notes

Keys: space step, enter run/pause, r reset. Memory is 64 words, byte-addressed (0..252). ecall halts.

Model: registers read in EX with full forwarding from EX/MEM and MEM/WB (↯ marks a forwarded operand). A load followed immediately by a consumer stalls one cycle (· ·). Branches and jumps resolve in EX; the one wrong-path instruction behind them is squashed (X).
